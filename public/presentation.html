Real-World School Engineering

Zero-Wait Performance & Crash Resilience for Peak Admission Days

High Availability
⚡

< 1 Second Save Unlock

Even for a 30–40 minute counselling session (10–25 MB audio), clicking Stop Recording saves binary audio locally in milliseconds and immediately unlocks the Save button.

☁

Persistent Background Queue

Once saved, a persistent app-level worker uploads recordings one-by-one to Cloudflare R2 & updates Firestore in background while the counsellor serves the next parent.

🛡

Power & Wi-Fi Fault Tolerance

Periodic IndexedDB recovery snapshots preserve active meetings against browser crashes (Restore Meeting). Wi-Fi drops auto-retry queued uploads immediately upon reconnect.

Protection Layer Scenario Addressed Automated System Behavior
Active Mic Lock Counsellor forgets to stop mic before clicking Save Save, Cancel & Navigation buttons stay locked with a 1-click "Go to Stop Recording ↑" jump button.
IndexedDB Local Vault Browser localStorage 5 MB quota limit exceeded Full binary audio blobs stay safely in IndexedDB (>100 MB capacity) while Firestore stores lightweight metadata.
Startup Recovery Sweep Laptop closed before background cloud sync finished On next login, the app automatically detects pending local recordings and finishes R2 cloud sync silently.

Technical Architecture

Smart Multi-Layer Cloud Architecture Designed for Minimum Cost

Enterprise Stack
System Layer Technology Used Purpose in Platform Cost & Quota Benefit
Real-Time Database Google Firebase Firestore Stores enquiry forms, tickets, follow-ups, transcripts, staff logins & review statuses. Ultra-low document size (<50 KB/lead); operates nearly within free/minimal tier.
Audio Media Vault Cloudflare R2 Object Storage 100% dedicated binary storage for `.webm/.mp3/.wav` parent meeting recordings. 10 GB Free Monthly + ₹0 Download/Egress Fees (huge savings over AWS/Firebase).
Local Speed Cache Browser IndexedDB Vault Instant local storage during recording, crash snapshots & zero-wait playback cache. Zero bandwidth cost for immediate replays on same desk.
AI Speech & Audit Google Gemini Audio & Text APIs Multilingual voice-to-text transcription, transcript structuring & quality coaching audit. Supports Hybrid Cost Mode (keeps High-Clarity Audio + Lite Text Summaries).
Optional Archive Google Drive & MongoDB Secondary institutional backup of meeting recordings & `.txt` transcript files. Uses existing school Google Workspace / free-tier replicas.
Why Separating Text (Firestore) & Audio (Cloudflare R2) Is Critical Storing 40-minute recordings in a traditional database would trigger expensive 1 MB limits and bandwidth fees. Keeping binary audio strictly in Cloudflare R2 cuts storage/playback egress costs to ₹0.
